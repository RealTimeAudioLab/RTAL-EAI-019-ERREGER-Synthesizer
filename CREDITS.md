## Upstream notices / Hinweise des Originals

The official homepage identifies its source link as GPL-3.0. Its version 1.19.8 credits also say that the synthesizer's own source will follow later. These statements do not establish the license of every reused file. The actual RTAL firmware was not supplied for this teaser and the exact upstream revision, file inventory and permissions remain unverified. No blanket license for the RTAL combined firmware is asserted.

Die offizielle Projektseite kennzeichnet den Quellcode-Link mit GPL-3.0. Die Credits der Version 1.19.8 kündigen den Quellcode des Synthesizers zugleich noch an. Daraus lässt sich nicht die Lizenz jeder tatsächlich übernommenen Datei ableiten. Für diesen Teaser lag keine Firmware zur Prüfung vor. Die genaue Originalrevision, der Dateibestand und die jeweiligen Rechte sind noch zu prüfen. Es wird keine pauschale Lizenz der RTAL-Gesamtfirmware behauptet.

Upstream credits additionally identify fm1-base (Charles Vestal and contributors) and felucca-bsp (Leo Kuroshita and contributors), as well as other third-party tools and libraries. Their upstream presence does not imply that RTAL uses them. The original factory banks credit Manuel Schleis, Alan Marcero and Sete; the original nine wavetables credit Sete. Upstream permission to distribute those assets with InfeKt-FM1 does not automatically establish permission to redistribute them with Erreger.

Die Credits des Originals benennen außerdem fm1-base (Charles Vestal und Mitwirkende), felucca-bsp (Leo Kuroshita und Mitwirkende) und weitere Drittkomponenten. Daraus folgt nicht, dass diese in RTAL enthalten sind. Die Original-Factory-Bänke nennen Manuel Schleis, Alan Marcero und Sete; die neun Original-Wavetables werden Sete zugeschrieben. Eine Erlaubnis zur Weitergabe mit InfeKt-FM1 gilt nicht automatisch für Erreger.

## Before distributing firmware / Vor Firmware-Veröffentlichung

1. Record the exact upstream revision and every reused source file and asset, including modifications and authorship notices.
2. Check the applicable license or obtain permission for each component. Preserve original headers and notices; comply with applicable corresponding-source obligations if distributing GPL-covered binaries or modifications.
3. Verify third-party factory patches and waves separately; exclude assets without confirmed redistribution rights.
4. Include the applicable unmodified license texts and notices in the actual source release. Select the repository license only after the audit.

This package contains only teaser documentation and five project-supplied GUI images, one enclosure image and one startup artwork, not firmware, presets, wavetables or copied upstream source files. A generic firmware LICENSE file is deliberately not included.
